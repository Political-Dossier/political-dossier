Plate VIIINotes on the Survey

How the map is drawn

Each party is drawn as its own country and each faction as a province. A province's size reflects how many organizations the survey places in it. Towns are organizations; their mark shows governing influence. Cross-party organizations sit on the islands between the two countries. Placement within a province is for legibility, not a measurement.

How organizations are scored

Every organization is scored 1 to 5 on seventeen dimensions shared by both parties, plus three kinds of influence. No organization gets a single left-right score. Scores are qualitative judgments from each group's public work, leadership and 2024-26 record, meant for comparison rather than measurement. Funding networks are not scored on ideology because their money crosses positions.

  • Intellectual influence. 5: its ideas are adopted by leaders across its party. 3: a regular voice in its faction. 1: niche audience.
  • Governing influence. 5: its alumni or founders hold top posts now, or it writes bills that pass. 3: regular access to members, staff or agencies. 1: little direct access.
  • Personnel influence. 5: a documented staffing pipeline at scale. 3: a recognizable alumni network. 1: none to speak of.

Changes from the original brief

  • Committee to Unleash Prosperity sits with the business and supply-side right, not the economic nationalists.
  • Niskanen and the Institute for Progress sit on the bridge isles.
  • Welcome is treated as electoral infrastructure, not a policy shop.
  • Heritage sits in America First territory after Project 2025 and the 2025 split.
  • Evergreen Freedom Foundation now operates as the Freedom Foundation.
  • Added the money, labor and media layer, groups that rose in 2025-26, and the Democratic legal and talent groups needed to compare capacity.

Caveats

Founding years and leaders follow public sources as of November 2026; where a current leader was not verified, the entry says so. Funding is listed only where publicly reported.
