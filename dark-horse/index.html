What counts as a dark horse

Someone with credible 2028 presidential speculation (early-state trips, a PAC or exploratory committee, public statements, poll inclusion or steady coverage) who isn't a front runner. Front runners poll consistently around 5% or higher nationally or appear on most outlets' shortlists. People who have ruled out a run stay on the map with a dashed outline while polls and coverage keep including them.

Declared Exploring: says publicly they're considering it Positioning: visible moves, no statement Speculative: talk from others Ruled out Bubbles take their party's color; the stronger the fill, the more serious the signals.
